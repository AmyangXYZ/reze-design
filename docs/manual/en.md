## The scene

Drop a `.pmx` folder or a `.zip` to load a model. Drop a `.vmd` for motion and an audio file for sound.

The **Scene** tab holds lighting, grade, background and camera. The **Materials** tab groups a model's materials and gives each group a shader graph.

Everything runs in your browser on WebGPU. Nothing is uploaded while you work.

## Presets

Grades, background effects and shader graphs live in libraries. Edit one and it becomes your draft, saved locally.

Publishing a draft shares it. Each publish is a new immutable version, so scenes already using it never change under them.

## Publishing a scene

**Share** packs the models, motions and audio you uploaded into one archive and publishes the whole look at a permanent link. Credits are required — name the artists behind the model, motion and music.
